Your Fear Is Not Your Final Answer

Your hands are shaking, and you still have to walk in there.

Most of us think fear means we're not ready. Usually it means the moment matters to you.

Don't wait for the fear to leave before you move. Take the next step while it's still in the room with you.

Scripture

"This is my command—be strong and courageous! Do not be afraid or discouraged. For the LORD your God is with you wherever you go." — Joshua 1:9 (NLT)

Practice for today

Say the fear out loud, then name one thing you can do in the next ten minutes.
