Stay in the Fight a Little Longer

Quitting always looks reasonable at the end of a long week.

It sounds like wisdom when you're tired. Before you walk away, ask one question: will I be glad I stopped, or only glad I was done?

Your answer might be to rest. It might be to finish one more thing.

Scripture

"So let's not get tired of doing good. At the right time we will reap a harvest of blessing if we don't give up." — Galatians 6:9 (NLT)

Practice for today

Pick one small thing you'll finish today, and finish it.
