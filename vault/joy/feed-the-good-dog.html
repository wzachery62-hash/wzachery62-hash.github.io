Joy · Anchor: The Positive Dog by Jon Gordon

Feed the Good Dog

Every day you feed one of two dogs inside you.

You don't always notice which one you're feeding. A kind word, a laugh with a friend, or a quiet moment outside feeds the good one.

Replaying an argument in the car or the bad news from this morning feeds the other. You get to choose today's meal.

Scripture

"Finally, my dear brothers and sisters, fix your thoughts on what is true, and good, and right. Think about things that are pure and lovely, and dwell on the admirable things." — Philippians 4:8 (NLT)

Practice for today

Find one thing today that made you smile, and say it out loud to someone.
