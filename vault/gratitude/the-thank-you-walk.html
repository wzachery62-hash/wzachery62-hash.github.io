The Thank-You Walk

You can walk past the same people every day and never really see them.

The neighbor who waves. The cashier who says your name. The person who keeps your street clean.

Try a different walk today. Stop by one of them and say thank you, specifically. Mean it, and say what they did. You might be surprised how much it changes the moment.

Scripture

"Give thanks in all circumstances; for this is God's will for you in Christ Jesus." — 1 Thessalonians 5:18 (NLT)

Practice for today

Thank one person today, and name the exact thing they did.
